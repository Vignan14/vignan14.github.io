About

The story so far

I've spent the last decade inside the engine room of enterprise learning — administering platforms, resolving escalations, and keeping global programs running for thousands of learners. My path runs through Standard Chartered, HCL, Deloitte and Accenture into PepsiCo, where I now lead a team of six delivering learning operations across North America. Along the way I earned an instructional design certification and a real appreciation for how people, process and technology combine to make learning actually work. Today I'm extending that foundation into learning systems, analytics and AI-enabled operations — the next chapter of the same craft.
